Runbooks

Step-by-step recovery procedures and engineering standards for this cell.

Most used

RB-01 · Edge node total loss

Isolate the node, drain traffic to the nearest peer, verify uplink optics, then re-image from the golden bundle.

RB-04 · Thermal runaway on rack

Force fan curve to max, shed non-critical workloads, and gate deploys until core temps fall under 70 °C for 10 minutes.

RB-09 · Failed firmware signature

Never bypass the HSM gate. Re-bake from a clean checkout and open a P2 with the build attestation attached.
