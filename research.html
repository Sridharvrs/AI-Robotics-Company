Focus Areas

Six Frontiers We Explore

Perception

Neural radiance fields, event cameras, and multi-modal sensor fusion for robust 3D understanding.

Manipulation

Dexterous in-hand manipulation, deformable object handling, and contact-rich control.

Learning

Reinforcement from demonstration, sim-to-real transfer, and foundation models for robotics.

Planning

Long-horizon task planning, symbolic reasoning, and safe exploration under uncertainty.

Human-Robot

Intent inference, shared autonomy, and natural language interaction with machines.

Swarm

Decentralized coordination, emergent behavior, and scalable multi-robot systems.
